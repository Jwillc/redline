REDLINE

Every sound here is computed from a simulated engine: gas burning in the cylinders, valves opening, and pressure waves running up and down the intake and exhaust. No recordings. The car's torque comes from the same simulation.

Three straight courses (bumps, jumps, things to crash through) and an open lot. Take them at different speeds with different engines and see what survives: wheels bend and break off, bumpers and glass go, hard nose landings wound the engine until it misfires and dies.

Keyboard or gamepad. Best with headphones. Inspired by Joel Johnson's Engine Synthesizer.
